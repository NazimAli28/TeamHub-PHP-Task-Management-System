# TeamHub Employee User Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to the TeamHub Employee User Guide. This guide will help you understand how to view your assigned tasks, track your performance, and complete work efficiently.

---

## 1. Login Process
- Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost/teamhub/`
- Enter your employee username and password.
- Click "Login" to access your employee dashboard.

---

## 2. Navigation Overview
- **Dashboard**: View your performance statistics and charts.
- **Tasks**: See your assigned tasks and mark them as completed.
- Use the sidebar to navigate between pages.

---

## 3. Viewing Assigned Tasks
- Go to the **Tasks** page.
- View all tasks assigned to you with details like order number, work type, and allotted time.
- Monitor countdown timers for each task.
- Tasks may be paused or resumed by admins, affecting the timers.

---

## 4. Task Completion Workflow
- On the **Tasks** page, locate the task you want to complete.
- Click the **Mark Completed** button when finished.
- The system will calculate your points gained based on time used.
- Receive real-time notifications and sound alerts for task updates.

---

## 5. Dashboard Statistics and Charts
- Access the **Dashboard** page to view your performance.
- See today's and monthly statistics including tasks completed, points assigned, points gained, bonus points, and total earned.
- Review weekly and monthly charts for points and tasks.
- Monitor alerts for overdue or near-deadline tasks.

---

## 6. Real-Time Notifications
- Receive instant notifications for new task assignments.
- Get alerts for task completions and updates.
- Sound notifications accompany important events.
- Check the dashboard for overdue task warnings.

---

## 7. Troubleshooting
- Ensure your browser supports JavaScript for real-time features.
- Check for audio permissions if notifications are not playing.
- Refresh the page if updates are not appearing immediately.
- Contact your admin if you encounter login or access issues.
